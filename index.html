The Platform

One loop, watched without pause: detect, predict, assess.

01 — Detect

Early signs of complication

Multispectral imaging signals surface the subtle changes that precede infection or breakdown — days before they'd be visible on a chart or to our eyes.

02 — Predict

Healing trajectories

Every wound is modelled against its own expected recovery envelope, so a case that's drifting off-course is caught the moment it deviates — not at the next visit.

03 — Assess

Treatment outcomes

Quantified, image-backed measurement of whether a therapy is working — giving clinicians and payers an objective read on what's helping the wound close.
